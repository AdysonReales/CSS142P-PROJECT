Operations Research Findings & Recommendations

  • Scenario A (Channel Decoupling): Cuts cashier wait time from 13.02 min → 1.60 min (-87.7%) and reduces balking by more than half by separating delivery couriers from dine-in ordering. Cashier utilization normalizes from 97.7% to 82.8%.
  • Scenario B (Floating Expediter): Pre-ordering in the line cuts cashier transaction duration by 55%, lowering customer wait to 5.05 min (-61.2%) and balking to 17.8% with zero physical counter reconstruction.
  • Scenario C (Dynamic Peak Staffing): Reallocating 1 kitchen prep worker to dispatch relieves the assembly bottleneck, dramatically increasing peak throughput from 7.52 → 12.62 orders/hr (+67.8%).
  • Combined (Exploratory Policy): Yields the best holistic performance across the Savana Market unit: 1.48 min wait, 3.73% balking, and 12.73 orders/hr throughput.

Rigorous Operations Research Metric Formulations

Cashier Waiting Time (Wq)

Wq = (1 / N_served) ∑ (t_order_start,i − t_queue_enter,i)

Measures queue delay strictly before the register. Excludes food production and dining time.

Time-Weighted Queue Length (Lq)

Lq = (1 / T_window) ∫ Lq(t) dt = ∑ [Lq_k × Δt_k] / T

Exact piecewise constant time-integral of visible waiting patrons. Not a single instantaneous snapshot.

Resource Utilization (ρ)

ρ = [Busy Resource-Minutes] / [Total Available Minutes × Capacity]

Evaluated for cashier staff, grill pit, assembly flex station, and dining tables.

Courier Dwell Time

Dwell = t_departure,courier − t_arrival,courier

Total time GrabFood / Foodpanda delivery riders occupy space at the Savana unit.

Documented Assumptions & Engineering Boundaries

  • Pre-warmed Grill Buffer: Initial pre-warmed buffer of 12 chicken pieces at 11:30 AM simulation start. Maximum grill capacity is modeled at 24 skewers based on hearth dimensions.
  • Dining Room Tables: Modeled as exactly 12 tables (6 in Left Dining Room, 6 in Right Dining Hall) matching the physical floor plan.
  • Delivery Courier Balking: Contracted delivery couriers do not balk (contractual fulfillment obligation). Walkaways are restricted to dine-in and takeout patrons.
  • Staffing Capacity: Baseline cashier count = 1. Scenario A express cashier = 1. Dynamic flex staffing reallocates 1 prep staff to dispatch during peak rush (12:00 – 1:00 PM).
